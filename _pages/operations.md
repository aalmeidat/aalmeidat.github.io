The data I use in my research come from surveys I have helped run. I spent six years at Las Campanas Observatory with APOGEE-2S, most of them as its Lead Observer, and then worked on commissioning, operations, and data quality for SDSS-V.

## APOGEE-2S, Las Campanas Observatory (2015–2021)

- **Lead Observer from fall 2016**, before the survey began in 2017: 232 survey nights on the 2.5-m du Pont telescope (2017–2021).
- Scheduled and supervised nine observers, reported survey progress and issues to the collaboration weekly, and was the first line of hardware and software troubleshooting during observing.
- Wrote the operator safety plan and the observer training and certification program, and trained and certified incoming observers.
- Commissioning of the APOGEE-South spectrograph (2017): prepared the survey hardware (plug-plate cartridges and the mapper station) and worked on the observer side throughout the spectrograph commissioning.
- During the COVID-19 pandemic, adapted operations so that part of the team observed remotely while a skeleton crew stayed on site.

## SDSS-V (2020–2025)

- **Deputy Commissioning Scientist, South (2020–2021):** helped shape and optimize the Las Campanas commissioning plan, and supported the test campaign of the focal-plane viewing camera for the robotic fiber positioners (2020).
- **Operations advising and data quality (2021–2025):** inspected survey data from both hemispheres daily and reported instrument, pipeline, and observing anomalies to the responsible teams.
- Data Release 18, the first SDSS-V data release ([Almeida et al. 2023, ApJS, 267, 44](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2023ApJS..267...44A); alphabetical collaboration author list).

## Instrument servicing

- **Las Campanas, 2025:** troubleshot the APOGEE cold shutter, replaced the APOGEE fiber link, and wrote and documented the replacement procedure.
- **Apache Point, 2024 shutdown:** supported primary-mirror cleaning, baffle reinstallation, and camera remounting, and contributed to the engineering documentation.
- **Apache Point, 2021:** APOGEE spectrograph servicing, including diagnosis and repair of internal mechanical subsystems, and clean-room reconditioning.

## Observing record

About 250 professional nights: 232 APOGEE-2S survey nights on the du Pont telescope (2017–2021), 15 photometry nights on the Swope telescope (2012–2014), 2 nights with IMACS on the 6.5-m Magellan/Baade telescope (2017), and observing support at APEX for Chilean time (2015).
